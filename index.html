Smart Switches With No Neutral Wire, For Indian Homes

Most Indian switchboards don't carry a neutral wire. Here's the honest version of what works — plus a 20-second tool to find your exact fit, and the tradeoff the manufacturers' lists quietly skip.

SHORT ANSWER
Yes — smart switches that work without a neutral wire exist and are sold in India. They run by drawing a tiny current through whatever's connected to them. Fine for most fans and decent-wattage lights, but they can make low-wattage LEDs flicker or faintly glow when "off." If you have the choice, a neutral-wired switch is more reliable. 01Why this is even a problem

In most Indian homes — especially anything built before the last few years — the switchboard only carries the live wire and the switched live running up to your light or fan. The neutral usually goes straight to the light fitting, bypassing the switch box entirely.

A normal switch doesn't care — it just breaks the live wire. But a smart switch is a tiny always-on computer. Its Wi-Fi or Zigbee radio needs a trickle of power even when the light is off, and traditionally needs both live and neutral to get it cleanly. No neutral in the box = the single most common reason a smart switch won't work in an Indian home.

02The tradeoff the "best switch" lists skip

The part that isn't flattering to anyone selling switches: a no-neutral smart switch is a compromise, not a free lunch. Because it siphons a small current through the load to stay alive, three things can happen:

  • LED flicker or ghost-glow — low-wattage LEDs may flicker or glow faintly even when "off," because the trickle current is enough to light them slightly.
  • Minimum-load needs — some no-neutral switches need the connected light/fan to draw above a minimum wattage; a single 5W LED can fall below that floor.
  • Bypass modules — the usual fix is a small capacitor wired in at the light: a ₹100–300 part and a slightly fiddlier install.

None of this makes them bad — it makes them a known quantity. If your box has no neutral and rewiring isn't practical, they're the right tool. You just go in with eyes open.

05FAQ

Can a smart switch work without a neutral wire?
Yes — no-neutral smart switches run on the live wire alone by trickling current through the load, with the LED-flicker caveat above.
Why don't most Indian homes have a neutral at the switch?
Traditional wiring routes neutral straight to the light fitting, leaving only live and switched-live at the switch box.
How do I know if my switch box has a neutral?
Power off, open the switch plate, and look for a bundle of wires (often black/blue) not connected to the switch terminals. Unsure? Ask an electrician — don't guess with live wiring.
Do no-neutral switches work with Alexa and Google Home?
Most do. Check the specific model's supported assistants, and prefer Matter-certified for the longest compatibility life.
Will a no-neutral switch make my LEDs flicker?
It can, especially with low-wattage LEDs. A small bypass module at the light fixes it in almost all cases.
